GREETING CARD

**DESCRIPTION:**   
 		A Simple paper made greeting card and it is constructed by           
basic electric component

**REQUIRED MATERIAL**:

* A4 paper/ card paper  
  *  Colour paper  
    * Scale  
    * Scissors  
    * Colour pens  
    * Glue  
    * Small LED light   
    * Aluminium foil paper  
    * Cello tape  
    * Battey 3v

**WORKING PRINCIPLE**  
             The greeting card uses a simple LED circuit powered by a coin cell battery. The LED is placed at the center of the layered heart design. When the LED terminals are connected to the battery terminals, electrical energy is converted into light energy. The emitted light illuminates the colourful heart layers, producing an attractive glowing effect. 

**PROCEDURE** 

1.   Fold an A4 sheet into a Greeting card   
2.   Cut heart shapes of different sizes using coloured papers  
3.   Arrange and paste the hearts from large to small to create the     layered design  
4. Make a small opening at a center and place the LED behind it   
5. Connect the LED to a coil cell battery and secure it with tape   
6. Decorate the card with small hearts and message   
7. Turn on the LED and check the glowing effect
